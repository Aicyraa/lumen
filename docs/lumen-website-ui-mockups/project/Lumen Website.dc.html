QUICKSTART — 04

Call it from your backend in a few lines.

Keep the key on your server. Your chatbot's frontend talks to your backend, and your backend talks to Lumen.

cURLNodePython
Copy
curl -X POST $LUMEN_URL/v1/chat \
  -H "x-api-key: $LUMEN_API_KEY" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"query": "When does enrollment close?"}'
