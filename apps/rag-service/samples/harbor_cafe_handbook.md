# Harbor Cafe Staff and Customer Handbook

## Opening hours
Harbor Cafe is open Monday to Friday from 7:00 in the morning until 6:00 in the evening. On Saturdays we open at 8:00 and close at 4:00. We are closed on Sundays and on public holidays, except for the Christmas market weekend, when the cafe opens from 10:00 to 14:00 on both days. The kitchen stops taking food orders thirty minutes before closing, while drinks can be ordered until the doors close. Early deliveries of milk and bread arrive between 6:00 and 6:45, so staff on the opening shift must be on site by 6:30 to receive them and to check the temperature of the fridge.

## Wi-Fi and laptops
Guests can use the free Wi-Fi. The network is called HarborGuest and the password is printed on the bottom of every menu card. The password changes on the first day of each month. Laptops are welcome at the tables along the left wall, which have power sockets. During the lunch rush between 12:00 and 13:30 we ask guests to move to the shared long table if they are working alone and the cafe is full. Video calls are fine with headphones.

## Allergies and dietary needs
Every dish on the menu lists its allergens in the small print under the description. The kitchen handles nuts, gluten, milk, eggs and sesame, so we cannot promise a completely allergen-free meal. Guests with a severe allergy must tell the server when ordering, and the server writes it in red on the ticket. The vegan options are the lentil soup, the roasted vegetable bowl and the oat milk porridge. Gluten-free bread is available for a small extra charge but it is toasted on the same machine as normal bread, so it is not safe for people with coeliac disease.

## Refunds and complaints
If a drink or dish is not what the guest ordered, we replace it for free or refund it in full, no questions asked. Complaints about food quality must be raised on the same day. For card payments the refund goes back to the same card and usually appears within five working days. Cash refunds are only possible with the original receipt. A manager must approve any refund above twenty euros, and the approval is written on the back of the till slip.

## Gift cards and loyalty stamps
Gift cards are sold at the counter in amounts of ten, twenty-five and fifty euros. A gift card is valid for two years after the purchase date and cannot be exchanged for cash. The loyalty card gives one stamp for every drink bought. After ten stamps the next drink is free, and a full card must be handed in at the till on the day it is used. Stamps are not given for gift card purchases or for takeaway water.

## Group bookings and events
Groups of eight or more people need to reserve a table at least two days ahead by calling the cafe or using the booking form on our website. For groups above fifteen we ask for a deposit of five euros per person, which is deducted from the final bill. The back room seats up to thirty guests and can be booked for private events on Sunday afternoons, when the cafe is otherwise closed. Bringing your own cake is allowed for birthdays, with a cake fee of two euros per slice.

## Staff rules
New staff complete a two-week trial period with a mentor. Uniform aprons are provided and washed by the cafe, but staff buy their own black non-slip shoes. Shift swaps must be agreed with the shift manager in writing at least twenty-four hours before the shift. Tips collected in the jar are split equally at the end of each week among everyone who worked that week. Staff drinks are free during the shift, but staff food is half price.

## Lost property and accessibility
Items left behind are kept in the box behind the counter for thirty days and then given to a local charity. The entrance has a step-free ramp on the harbor side, and one accessible toilet is located next to the kitchen. Guide dogs are always welcome. Menus in large print and in Braille are available on request at the counter.
